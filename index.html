One question, many places to look.

Answering a question about a patient can mean checking lab reports, drug labels, patient history and guidelines. Today's AI tools bring their own problems:

Where answers come from.

MediAssist searches the patient's reports and a medical knowledge base on the device first. It reaches public sources only when nothing local answers the question.

Tap an example to see which sources it uses. Grey ring: searched, no match. Filled: used for the answer.

Ask the way that suits the moment.

Questions can be typed, spoken, or asked about a photo or PDF of a report or medicine label. Everything is read on the device.

Only keywords leave the hospital.

Patient records never leave. When a public source is needed, MediAssist strips the question down to medical keywords and lets a local model pick one of four sources through the Model Context Protocol.

Data

Patients and reports are synthetic. The demo replays answers recorded in testing; the real app runs its models on the device.

What testing showed.

Tested on 5 synthetic lab reports in 4 formats, 40 source questions and 80 scenarios.

Scope. A research prototype tested on synthetic reports. It does not diagnose or give treatment advice, and it is not a medical device. A study with clinical staff is the next step.
